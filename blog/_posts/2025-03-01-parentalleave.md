So Sweden is well known for it's very generous parental leave, and it's true, the system is *very* generous and I'm so grateful to be raising our son here. BUT the system is a LOT more complicated than it appears at first glance and the cynic in me can't help but wonder if that's deliberate because there's a lot of little terms and conditions you have to look out for to really get the full benefits of those 480 days.

I tried to figure it out myself and almost had a complete mental breakdown, luckily my husband stepped in and sacrificed all his remaining, sleep-deprived brain cells and now finally, seven and a half months into my son's life, we know what the hell we are doing with our parental leave.
